Lynqo Privacy Policy

Lynqo keeps your items on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no advertising.

Last updated: October 18, 2026
Terms of Use Help & Support

Summary

The Lynqo app does not collect personal data. Everything you create stays on your device, and the developer has no access to it. AI helpers run on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence.

Who We Are

Lynqo is developed and published by Aliaksandr Mazurau, a sole trader, who is responsible for this app and for any personal data described in this policy. Contact: au.mazurau@gmail.com

What Stays on Your Device

  • Items you create: titles, notes, item type, amounts, due dates, and reminder dates.
  • The original input you used with an AI helper: rough text, a voice transcript, or a screenshot you chose to attach.
  • App preferences, such as whether you finished onboarding.

This data is stored locally on your iPhone. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party. If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, iOS may include Lynqo's data in that backup, as with any other app.

AI Helpers (Smart Capture and Voice + AI)

Smart Capture and Voice + AI use Apple's on-device Foundation Models to turn your input into one editable item. This processing happens on your iPhone. Lynqo does not send your notes, transcripts, or screenshots to any server.

Voice and Speech Recognition

If you use voice capture, Lynqo asks for microphone and speech recognition permission. Your recording is kept only until it has been turned into text and is then deleted. Lynqo uses Apple's speech recognition, on device whenever your iPhone supports it. If on-device recognition is not available for your language, iOS may send the audio to Apple to transcribe it, under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer never receives your audio or transcripts.

Screenshots and Photos

Lynqo only sees an image you pick yourself with the iOS photo picker. It does not get access to your photo library. Text in the image is read on your device to help fill the item, and the image is saved with that item so you can see the original later.

Reminders

Reminder alerts are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Lynqo Pro subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your name, email, or payment details. Lynqo only checks on your device, using Apple's StoreKit, whether your Apple Account has an active subscription. Apple handles purchase data under its own privacy policy.

Contacting Support

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, your message, and anything you choose to attach, such as a screenshot. This is used only to answer you and fix the problem you report. It is not used for marketing or shared with anyone, apart from the email provider that delivers the message. Support emails are deleted once they are no longer needed, and no later than two years after the conversation ends. The legal basis is the developer's legitimate interest in answering your request (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

What Lynqo Does Not Do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no sale or sharing of data.
  • No server that stores your content.

Keeping and Deleting Your Data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete any item in the app. Deleting Lynqo from your iPhone removes all of its data from the device. Backups follow your iCloud or computer backup settings.

Your Rights

The app does not send your data to the developer, so the content you create is under your control on your device. If you have emailed support, you can ask for a copy of that correspondence or for it to be deleted. If you have a privacy question or request, including under the GDPR, write to au.mazurau@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Lynqo is a general productivity app and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. If an update ever introduces data collection, it will be described here and in the App Store privacy details before it ships.
