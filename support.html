Lynqo Help & Support

Lynqo turns tasks, reminders, follow-ups, notes, and expenses into saved items fast. Type them yourself, or let the AI helpers fill them from a rough note, your voice, or a screenshot.

Privacy Policy Terms of Use

Getting Started

  • Tap + on the timeline to create an item yourself. This is free.
  • Use Voice + AI on the timeline to say what you need. Lynqo fills one item for you to review.
  • Use Smart Capture in New Item to paste a rough note or attach a screenshot, such as a chat, receipt, or invoice.
  • Review the suggested fields, adjust anything, and save. Saved items live on your timeline, where you can edit, complete, or delete them.

AI Helper Requirements

Smart Capture and Voice + AI run on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence. They need:

  • An iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, running iOS 26 or later.
  • Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri.
  • A device and Siri language that Apple Intelligence supports.
  • Apple Intelligence models downloaded. Right after you turn it on, this can take a while on Wi-Fi.

Lynqo shows what is missing in Settings > Status & Setup. You can always create and manage items yourself.

Lynqo Pro

  • Lynqo Pro unlocks Smart Capture and Voice + AI, billed monthly or yearly. New subscribers can get a 7-day free trial, once per Apple Account.
  • Restore: on a new iPhone or after reinstalling, open Lynqo > Settings > Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Cancel or change plan: Lynqo > Settings > Manage Subscription, or iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid being charged.
  • Refunds: Apple processes payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Permissions

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition are needed only for voice capture.
  • Notifications are needed only for reminder alerts.
  • Screenshots are chosen with the iOS photo picker, so Lynqo never needs access to your whole library.

If you denied a permission, Lynqo explains what is unavailable and can open iOS Settings for you.

Reminders

Reminder items save even with notifications off, but they won't alert you. To get alerts, allow notifications for Lynqo, check that Focus doesn't silence it, and confirm the date and time before you leave the item.

Your Data

Your items stay on your iPhone. There is no account and no sync between devices. iCloud Backup or a computer backup can include them, and deleting the app removes them. See the Privacy Policy for details.
